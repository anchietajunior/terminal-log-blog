## José Anchieta dos Santos Júnior

**Software Engineer** · **Consultant** · **Professor**

---

Product-minded software engineer, consultant and professor with over 15 years of experience. I believe great software comes from deeply understanding the domain and the people who use it — code is just the medium. My focus has always been on solving real business problems, not just shipping features. Specialist in Ruby on Rails, with strong expertise in system design, domain modeling, performance optimization, and leading technical teams. I teach because explaining things well forces me to understand them better. Currently pursuing a deeper understanding of Artificial Intelligence — not just using AI tools, but studying the math, the models, and the theory behind them. I want to understand *why* things work, not just *how* to use them. That's what drives my master's research in Intelligent Computing and my ongoing studies in Software Engineering with AI.
